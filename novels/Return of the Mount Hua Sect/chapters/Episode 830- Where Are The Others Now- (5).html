How could the Justice Faction and Evil Faction make peace?

“Oh my god… who would have thought that Shaolin, Wudang, and the Namgung family would abandon the civilians and find their own way… so what was the deal they had been clamoring for so far? And yet they pride themselves on being the Justice Faction?”
“Why have we supported them until now! If this is the case, aren’t they no different from the thieves?”
“Those guys are fucking scammers, scammers! In the first place, they were just people who were only interested in money and gains! We got fooled. Entirely fooled!”
Anger began to spread.
People naturally felt more anger when they were betrayed by those they trusted than when wronged by those known for evil. As great as the trust placed in the Nine Great Sects and Five Great Families was, the disappointment and anger over their choices were enormous.
Once the rumor spread from the Yangtze River to each province, it quickly engulfed the entire central plain like wildfire.
More than a hundred years after the war against the Demonic Sect, the most shocking news shook the quiet Central Plains like an earthquake.
