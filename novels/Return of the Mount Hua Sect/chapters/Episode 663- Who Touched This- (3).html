The pure white paws, which were so small that one would call them cute, were pointed down. And the dogs, all being sorted by size, fell to the ground.
Wheik!
When the small paw moved up, the dogs rose up in speed, and then the paw drew a circle, and the dogs rolled on the ground.

“Am I dreaming now?”
If one hadn’t seen it with their own eyes, they wouldn’t even believe this, but those who climbed Mount Hua were realizing that what they were witnessing might be difficult.
Every time the little marten, which seemed like a bundle of white fur, moved, the dogs as big as people turned.
They were worried about something happening if the dogs moved too much.
“Ah, no, it doesn’t look like it is worth it.”
“Is that a marten? Isn’t the problem why the dogs are listening to the marten?”
“I find it odd that the marten is waving its paw at the dogs.”
Something, from the start of it to the end, made no sense.
Hik!
When the marten slightly showed its teeth in anger, all the dogs would have their tails curled in.
This pure white marten finally nodded as if to say this was right.

“There are spirit beasts in Mount Hua?”
“… but I thought those things only appeared in stories.”
Right now, the condition of the Central Plains was so bad that finding a spirit beast was unheard of, so everyone thought it was odd or found it unfamiliar.
Shhh!
When Baek Ah flipped her front paw to the side, the dogs rushed to the back of the pavilion. Everyone there was shocked and impressed, and some even applauded it for the training, which was neatly organized as if there were no dogs there at all.
“Is this the power of the Beast Palace?”
“What did you even see? That marten was raised by the Mount Hua disciple!”
“Huh? But why?”
“… do you even understand?”
They really couldn’t understand what was happening, so everyone just went silent.
‘Since earlier, something felt so odd.’
‘I don’t really understand anything.’
The people began to realize that Mount Hua wasn’t like any other sect. But it felt absurd for Mount Hua, too.
“… it was handled.”

“Actually, the dogs aren’t the problem.”
Baek Cheon looked away, shocked. Chung Myung was barking at Meng So… no, protesting.
“Dog’s Palace! This place is entirely messed up! Right!”
“Hahahah! Do not worry about such small things! Now, since I was coming to meet you, I brought the alcohol from Yunnan! Wouldn’t you like a refreshing drink?”

“You will be so drunk that you’ll be happy to die! I had a hard time getting them! How is it? Now, will you like a drink?”

Such a sad thing for Mount Hua. Here, human-like animals and animal-like humans coexisted.

“Now that the Beast Palace has arrived, I am going to select a couple of my friends and send them down to the village.”

“Yes. Not everyone who comes to Mount Hua can stay here, so we will need people to open up temporary tents, which I mentioned earlier, and manage them.”

“Even if this is Mount Hua and not the thing of the alliance, Mount Hua and the Tang family aren’t strangers.”

“So, Soso.”

“Others are all busy working and you two seem to be having a great time?”

Even without an introduction, they could recognize that this man was the Lord of a Palace.
On one side was the Nanman Beast Palace Lord.
On the other side was the head of the Sichuan Tang family, the ruler of Sichuan, and the Sect Leader of Mount Hua, the man who gained immense power recently.
Everyone gulped at the thought of these three shaking up the world.
“What is that?”
“Is something happening?”
A glance was enough to know that Meng So was strong. To say that he looked like a beast didn’t fit; wasn’t he an outsider, unknown to the manners of the Central Plains?
Everyone was tense, wondering what would happen next.
And after a while, Hyun Jong smiled and bowed.
“Welcome to Mount Hua, Palace Lord. I have heard so much about the Nanman Beast Palace and the stories about the Palace Lord from Lord Tang and my disciples. I should have come to greet you right then, but I couldn’t. Please forgive my lack of courtesy.”
Meng So, seeing such a humble form, snorted.
It was right to respond to a hit with the same force. But instead of being polite, Meng So chose another method.
Thud!
He took a step back and bowed deeply.
“Uh?”
“What?”
Everyone was shocked at that.
Reaching out to the same level of manners was nice. But that Meng So now didn’t do that and lowered his head.
‘Is he saying he is going to recognize Mount Hua as the better sect?’
‘Eh, even so, it cannot….’
Not everyone could understand the actions. But then Meng So spoke in a loud voice.
“Great!”
Great? What great?
“It is my honor to meet the sect leader of the Great Mount Hua Sect!”
Those watching were all shocked.
‘W-what?’
‘What does this mean?’
The Nanman Beast Palace.
Of course, the people of the Central Plains tended to secretly refuse or ignore the sects outside their lands. And actually, it was true that the strength of the palace didn’t reach that of the Nine Great Sects. But still, the Nanman Beast Palace was a member of the five palaces.
Even though Mount Hua’s recent feats were amazing, it was still not a sect that dared to approach the Nanman Beast Palace.
But now, in front of their eyes, the Beast Palace Lord, Meng So, was showing extreme respect to Mount Hua’s sect leader.
Even Hyun Jong felt a bit embarrassed.
“Ahem. Palace Lord. You are showing excessive kindness.”
“No!”
However, Meng So raised his head and shouted.
“I apologize for only coming to meet you now. On behalf of the Nanman Beast Palace and all those who live in Yunnan! I am grateful for the grace of the Plum Blossom Sword Saint who protected Yunnan, and I am even more grateful to Mount Hua for teaching the Plum Blossom Sword Saint!”
Meng So placed his hands in front of his chest and bowed deeply once again. Although his manner was different from what the Central Plains people would do, it was obvious to anyone that he was showing respect.
As a result, Meng So’s words from earlier became more credible.
“The Plum Blossom Sword Saint saved Yunnan…?”
“Who is the Plum Blossom Sword Saint?”

The people around were confused.
But before they could talk and discuss, Meng So shouted loudly.
“Nothing can repay the grace of saving a life! Nanman Beast Palace will be the eternal friend of Mount Hua and the enemies of Mount Hua will be our enemies! Sect leader, Beast Palace will not betray it!”
Hyun Jong felt a stinging sensation at the tip of his nose, so he turned to the side and looked at the distant sky.
No one recognized them.
The reason why he had to keep the feeling of regret within his heart while seeing so many people come to Mount Hua was because he thought no one knew about the past of Mount Hua.
But now, wasn’t Meng So bowing down in front of everyone and mentioning the name of Mount Hua and the Plum Blossom Sword Saint?
“… it was so long ago. There…..”

“Save the Beast Palace, save Yunnan! Furthermore, what does time have to do with us discussing the grace that the Plum Blossom Sword Saint showed when saving the world from the clutches of those wicked, evil Demonic Sects!”
“….”
“That is something which cannot be forgotten for a thousand years to come, let alone in a hundred years!”

“I brought a nice gift from Yunnan! We have a lot to talk about, too! So let us head in.”

A strange silence.
