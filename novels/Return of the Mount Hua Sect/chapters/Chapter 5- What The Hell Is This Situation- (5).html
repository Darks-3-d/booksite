(5)\n‘What is that?’\nChung Myung shook his head to clear his mind, only to find something shiny in front of him.\n‘*Huh?*’\nAt the same time, the sound of metal clicking could be heard.\n“*Tsk Tsk Tsk*. He’s still so young. How did he end up as a beggar?”\n*Clink.*\n*Clink.*\nCoins started flying towards Chung Myung.\n“It looks like he’s been beaten pretty badly; looks like he’ll die soon.”\n“Such a terrible world. What a pitiful kid.”\n‘What?’\nWhat was happening…\n‘*Ah*.’\n‘That’s right, I’m a beggar, aren’t I? I forgot for a second.’\nTo everyone else, Chung Myung looked like a beggar. No, he was a beggar in his own eyes too. He was a young child who hadn’t reached adulthood yet. He was so severely beaten that his eyes were puffy and blue while blood clotted his open wounds…\nAnd such a beggar was lying flat on the street.\n“*Ugh*. He can’t survive without our help.”\n“I can’t just watch a child suffer like that without help. What sort of person would do such a thing?”\nA raggedy young beggar, with underdeveloped limbs and covered in dust, seemed to be dying.\nNo, it didn’t merely look like he was dying; Chung Myung was really dying.\nHow else could such a perfect beggar scene be witnessed from him?\nCoin kept flying into view, one after another.\n“*Tsk. Tsk*.”\n“No matter how I look at him, he’s just a beggar. How did he get here?”\nIt seemed like the world still had kindness in it.\nPeople were clicking their tongues as they passed by Chung Myung before tossing a few coins in his direction. Looking at the coins that came pouring in, Chung Myung couldn’t restrain the tears in his eyes.\n“Oh my! He’s crying. Poor thing, it sure is pitiful.”\n“Go and buy yourself some noodles with that money.”\nThe warmth was too much. However, the reason why Chung Myung was shedding tears wasn’t due to the kindness he received.\n‘A disciple of the Great Mount Hua Sect has been reduced to begging!’\nHow did he fall so low?\nUntil just a few days ago, he was the greatest swordsman in the world. He risked his life and went to battle Chun Ma, who was about to usher in a new era of chaos. Before that, he was the most elegant swordsman that roamed the world.\nBut now, he was here begging. The world was finding new ways to wound him!\n‘I have some pride too!’\nIf he accepted the money, then he would truly turn into a beggar. What a beggar he would be, a noble swordsman of the great Mount Hua who earned his living clutching the ankles of others. As if he would actually pick up the money! It was a truly impossible task for Chung Myung.\nChung Myung had a warrior’s pride. He would never give up. Even when there was a blade about to slit his throat, he refused to bend or break!\nChung Myung raised his head while clenching his teeth and shouted!\n“Thank you so much! You will be blessed, sir!”\nHis pride was long dead.\nHe had to live.\n“*Kyaaaa~!*”\nChung Myung patted his bulging belly. He looked weird, like a tadpole with a slim body that held no charm and a convex belly.\n“Self~ esteem~ went to~ drain~ with the food. Better to live as a beggar~ than die!”\nDue to his small stature, he couldn’t eat much.\nJust three dumplings were enough to make his stomach bulge out. He still had three coins left up his sleeve.\nCoins that he would have never picked up in his past life looked like treasures in this new life.\n‘I never would have thought that money was so important.’\nChung Myung remembered the words of his Sahyung, who would always hold his head in agony on the last day of each month.\n– Warriors need to be obsessed with wealth!\nIf possible, Chung Myung wanted to go back to those days and ease his Sahyung’s suffering!\n‘My stomach is actually full.’\nA young beggar was mouthing his complaints off to a ghost.\nChung Myung had to keep himself from crying when he thought of his hard-working, money-obsessed, Sahyung’s final moments in the battle with .\n‘Anyway.’\n“First, I need to live.”\nChung Myung scratched his head.\nHe left without a plan, and he had no idea how to get to Shaanxi.\n‘I underestimated how far away 2,000 miles is.’\nChung Myung began to reflect on his actions. All of his plans revolved around the abilities of his previous body. Even though he knew that his body and the circumstances had changed, it wasn’t an easy task to adapt to his new body overnight.\nIn the past, he would’ve reached Shaanxi in half a day. He would have even stopped for a cold drink at an inn beneath Mount Hua, but now, heading to Mount Hua with his deprived body was a risky venture.\nEven if Chung Myung could defeat any mountain bandits or beasts, there was no way he could overcome hunger.\nHis mind felt burdened as he realized that the journey, he expected to be easy, turned into an epic journey that could cost him the life he had just regained.\n‘But I can’t just beg my way to Shaanxi.’\nChung Myung scratched his head, feeling lost.\n“I need to find a way, but I need to understand what I need to find a way!”\nHe wasn’t aware of it when he first awoke, but a hundred years had passed since his era. It was no exaggeration to say that Chung Myung had no idea how the current world worked. But… how should he go about finding out?\n“Hey!”\nAn average person would take more than two months to get to Shaanxi. It wouldn’t be too strange to believe that, with Chung Myung’s frail body, it could take up to half a year to reach Mount Hua.\n“Hey!”\nIt was horrifying and frustrating for Chung Myung, who desperately wanted to see Mount Hua’s condition with his own eyes. He had to find a way, but right now, not a single thing came to his mind…\n“*Hey*, you bastard! You deaf or something?”\n“*Huh?*”\nChung Myung turned his head.\n‘What now?’\nHe heard everything clearly, but he didn’t think that someone would be calling for him. There was no reason why anyone would talk to a beggar.\nThree beggars were glaring down at him with ugly faces.\n‘They look like beggars.’\nHis current life seemed to be intertwined with beggars in many ways.\n“Me?”\n“You? Who else could I be calling? What the hell is wrong with you!?”\nThe ugly beggar standing in front of Chung Myung spat on the floor.\n“I don’t know what hole you crawled out from, but who gave you permission to beg here?”\nWas permission needed to beg?\n“You’re still a child, so I’ll spare your life. Take out everything you have, including the money you have hidden in your sleeve, and leave this place.”\nThe remainder of Chung Myung’s limited funds were stashed in his sleeves and in his hands…\nChung Myung looked down at his hand. They were the leftovers, the cold dumplings.\n“… you people have no conscience. You bastards!”\nTake this away? This little bit of food too? What was he supposed to eat!?\n“You beggar!”\nWhoa, a beggar was cursing at another beggar for being a beggar. It felt like he was spitting on his own face.\n“Wait a minute.”\nChung Myung carefully placed the dumplings to the side.\nHe then stood up, stretched his hand forward a couple times, took a step ahead, then one step behind, and repeated that several times.\nThe beggars, who were watching the strange scene, rolled their eyes.\n“What are you doing now?”\n“*Ah*. Wait just a moment. I’ll be done soon.”\nAfter stretching his arms and legs a couple more times, Chung Myung got up.\n“It’s not entirely perfect, but this should be enough.”\nAfter his odd routine, Chung Myung had managed to get a sense of distance and understand the length of his reach. Even with his short limbs, he managed to adapt.\nSo!\n“You guys are about to be very upset; I have a grudge against beggars.”\n“… What?”\n“Don’t you understand what I’m saying? Well, that’s just how the world is. It’s messed up. Don’t be too pissed off; just accept it and move on.”\n“What is this bastard talking about!?”\nChung Myung cracked his neck.\n“*Ah*. By the way, I can’t hit very hard because my body is weak. So, you’re going to get hit a whole lot to make up for it.”\n“This crazy…”\nAt that moment, Chung Myung lunged ahead and punched the face of the beggar standing at the front.\n*Ack!*\nA proper step. A fully extended fist that gathered strength through the gentle rotation of the waist utilizing strongly grounded legs.\nChung Myung’s fist slammed right into the beggar’s face, and the sound was like slamming a bamboo stick against a wall.\n*Thud!*\nThe beggar who got hit with the unexpected blow silently collapsed on the spot.\nSeeing that, Chung Myung smiled.\n“You!”\n*Puck!*\nHe kicked another beggar.\n“I am Plum Blossom Sword Saint of the Great Mount Hua! You bastards!”\nAt that moment, all his accumulated resentment after reincarnation exploded.\n“… esteemed sir.”\n“Does a beggar look like an esteemed sir?”\n“Great beggar…?”\n“Should I hit you again?”\nThe beggars, who had their asses handed to them, whined.\n‘Where did this monster come from?’\n‘It hurts so much that I could die.’\nA couple hits were all it took for Chung Myung to subdue these beggars. It was shocking that a child knocked out three grown adults, and it was clearly a disastrous result for the three of them.\n‘It’s going to take some time to get used to this body.’\nIt didn’t take much to subdue them. But if he had been in his previous body, he would have turned them into rice cakes with just one finger.\nBut now, he had to kick, run, roll and even punch to subdue the beggars. One of them kept getting up after every hit, so Chung Myung had to slap him 38 times.\n‘That Jong Pal…Cho Pal? …Whatever it was, that bastard should also be taught like this.’\nJust thinking about him made Chung Myung gnash his teeth.\nIf only he had known how short his new limbs were in advance! If only he had the stamina to get up after being beaten, he could have beaten Pal Cho.\nHe was in a hurry to get back to Mount Hua, so he couldn’t even take revenge. One day, however, one day he would definitely catch that beggar and teach him a lesson.\n“Men.”\n“Yes!”\nThe three beggars jumped up. Sweat trickled down their face. All three of them looked at Chung Myung.\n‘No matter how I see it, he looks like a beggar who couldn’t even lift a bag of rice.’\n‘This makes no sense!’\nTotally incomprehensible.\nSmall, weak, and thin.\nOn the surface, he looked weak and frail; no one expected that he could fight so fiercely. So, when they got close, they ended up seeing stars. Their fists never even touched Chung Myung’s clothes.\nChung Myung wasn’t very fast or strong, so why did they lose to him?\n“Hey!”\n“Yes!”\n“Please give your orders! Esteemed sir! No, esteemed beggar! *Ah*, no!”\nWhat’s the point in understanding? It takes time to convince someone but beating them is quick. The only important thing was that the young beggar’s punches hurt like hell.\nChung Myung glanced at the beggars and opened his mouth.\n“I am just asking; do you know how to reach Shaanxi quickly?”\nThe beggars looked at each other and smiled. Chung Myung began to feel like there was some hope when he saw their expressions.\n“*Hahaha.* You are asking something obvious.”\n“*Oh?* Really?”\n“If we knew something like that, why would we be begging? At least ask something worthwhile.”\n“…”\nChung Myung looked at the beggars and sighed deeply.\nThese guys weren’t wrong. At the very least, it was his mistake to seek a path from a beggar.\n“Enough. Go away.”\n“Thank you!”\n“Be strong!”\nThe beggars bowed their heads and turned around, ready to run away. No, they tried to run away.\n“Hold it.”\nAt that moment, Chung Myung’s words froze them in place.\n“Yes?”\n“It is fine to flee but take out everything in your hands and pockets and leave them here.”\n“…”\n“You came up behind me while I was eating, so one coin… no, ten coins. Trust me, it is better than getting hit again.”\nRealizing how sincere he sounded, the beggars relinquished the coins in their pockets and quickly escaped.\n“*Ugh*, you.”\n“Yes?”\n“Take off everything aside from your underwear before I take them off for you.”\n“…”\nIt was a horrific scene where a beggar was being robbed in daylight.
