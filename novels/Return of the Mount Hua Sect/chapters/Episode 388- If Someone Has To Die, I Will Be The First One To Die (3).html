Beggars Union served information.
And if they came here, it meant something wrong was happening.

“Given how they are moving, it seems like Mount Hua is their target.”

“Ten Thousand People Clan!”
The voices of the Mount Hua disciples were filled with despair and gasps.
A fear which couldn’t be hidden spread around.

Their opponent was that good. It was one of the Five Major Clans of the Evil Faction.
It was a place that could not be compared with Mount Hua because its reputation was that much greater.

“From what I know, doesn’t the clan have its own troops organized?”
“Yes, there are a total of twelve troops, but there are also several more groups and units besides them.”
“And three of them.”
It was a relief that the entire clan didn’t come here, but no one leading a clan would do that.
‘It must mean they thought three troops were enough to deal with Mount Hua. They are that confident.’
Mount Hua, on the other hand, didn’t even have their full power.
