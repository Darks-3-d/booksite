Return of the Mount Hua Sect > Chapter 77: Rolling Stone Gathers No Moss! (2)\n\nChapter 77: Rolling Stone Gathers No Moss! (2)\n‘What the hell is going on here?’\nJo Gul couldn’t come to his senses.\nIt was the first time Jo Gul saw her from such a close distance; he wondered if there could be any disciple in Mount Hua who didn’t know of Yu Yiseol.\nIt wasn’t because Jo Gul was a third-class disciple. He had never even heard of her having a friendly conversation with anyone. Despite being a disciple of Mount Hua, Yu Yiseol was known to keep her distance from others.\nBut now, she was talking to Chung Myung right in front of him. Soon, the piercing glares of the second-class disciples all stabbed into Chung Myung like razor-sharp daggers.\n‘Wow, those eyes could really kill people.’\nWell, it was all aimed towards Chung Myung, who was next to Jo Gul. But sitting next to him, it felt inevitable that he would be dragged into the crossfire.\n‘Chung Myung, please!’\nBe respectful, you bastard!\nThese are the second-class disciples; what is with that tone?\nDespite all that, Chung Myung remained calm. In an atmosphere that felt like something was about to happen, Yu Yiseol whispered softly as if nothing was wrong.\n“It’ll only be a minute.”\n“I said I’m not interested.”\n“But it really will just take a moment.”\n“I said no! Don’t you get what I am saying!?”\nWatch the way you talk! Speak politely, you bastard!\nJo Gul ended up pressing on Chung Myung’s thighs without realizing it, causing Chung Myung to turn his head toward him.\n“What?”\n“… *Uh*…”\nYou shouldn’t ask that!\nUh… he shouldn’t have asked that in front of his sasuks. He should understand what was happening.\nClicking his tongue, Chung Myung turned his head to Yu Yiseol.\n“I have nothing to say or speak about with you.”\n“But I have something to tell you.”\n“I really don’t want to hear it.”\n“It is fine, just one minute. Let’s go outside.”\n“No need, can’t you see I’m eating?”\n“Then, I’ll wait until you finish.”\nThe second-class disciples were on the verge of going crazy.\nFirst, they had never seen Yu Yiseol speak so much at once to a younger disciple. No, to be precise, they didn’t remember her ever talking so much with any of the second-class disciples either.\nBut, didn’t it seem like Yu Yiseol was clinging onto this man?\nRight. The second thing that made them agitated was the attitude of Chung Myung.\n‘That crazy son of a bitch….!’\n‘Nothing is going to happen now, right?’\n‘Doesn’t that bastard have eyes?’\n‘He has to be a true Taoist.’\nBurning anger and subtle respect were poured onto Chung Myung.\nAs a Taoist who cultivates and walks the path of Dao, the first thing one must do is stay away from worldly desires. However, Yu Yiseol’s existence was one that destroyed that dedication. No matter how well-disciplined a person was, they had no choice but to listen and stray from their path if Yu Yiseol made a request.\nAnyone would, seeing her beautiful face and expression.\nHowever, Chung Myung was openly complaining that Yu Yiseol was annoying him. That was unimaginable for the second-class disciples.\n“I said no! I have a lot of work to do. Find someone else.”\n“I came here for you.”\n“No, why would—”\nIt was then.\n“*Ahem*.”\nAlthough it was low, it was a sound that spread widely after being imbued with qi.\nEveryone turned their heads to see Baek Cheon, who steadily got up from his seat and smiled at Chung Myung.\n“Chung Myung, was it?”\n“Yes.”\nA short answer.\nBut Baek Cheon wasn’t angry. In such a situation, showing anger meant that the opponent won.\n“I understand that you’re tired from visiting the sect leader and taking on various tasks. You’re likely even doing work that I’m unaware of. However, since your senior is so kindly requesting your time, don’t you think you should hear what she has to say, regardless of how tired you may be?”\nBaek Cheon spoke softly to Chung Myung. He thought that it would work out…\n“Why?”\nBaek Cheon’s hand trembled.\nDid he hear wrong?\nNo? So why did he hear the word ‘why’?\nShocked, Baek Cheon looked at Chung Myung’s expression. Seeing that pouty expression, it didn’t seem like he heard it wrong.\nForcing the anger down to calm himself,\n‘Be calm.’\nIf he got angry at a third-class disciple, it would reflect negatively on his character.\n“Did you say ‘why’?”\n“Yes.”\n“There are many reasons. First of all, a person should have manners. There is also a rule which says that you need to respect those above you.”\n“*Ah*, manners?”\nAs if he understood, Chung Myung nodded and looked at Yu Yiseol.\n“Senior.”\n“Yes?”\n“Apologize quickly. He’s saying that you aren’t behaving politely right now.”\n“…”\nYu Yiseol raised a finger, pointing to herself.\n“Yes. Apologize.”\n“Me?”\n“*Ugh*, you don’t seem to understand at all.”\nChung Myung explained it.\n“This is a party to celebrate the return of the second-class disciples. It’s rude to make use of this gathering to sort out your personal affairs and act individually.”\n“*Ah*…”\nAs if that made sense, Yu Yiseol nodded her head, stood from her seat, and bowed to Baek Cheon.\n“I apologize, Sahyung. I didn’t think that far.”\n“Ah, no, Samae. That’s not—”\nBaek Cheon bit his lip.\nJust why did it turn out like this?\nAnd when he tried to open his mouth and manage the situation, Chung Myung spoke again.\n“Not to him, you should apologize to Sasuk, since you made a mistake around your senior.”\n“You’re right.”\nYu Yiseol bowed her head again towards Un Geom.\n“This disciple was short-sighted.”\nUn Geom smiled.\n“It is fine now that you understand, so sit down.”\n“Yes, Sasuk.”\nIt was Baek Cheon who ended up getting Yu Yiseol into trouble. He couldn’t figure out what to do.\nHe was flustered… and it was embarrassing to back down without seeing it through to the end. Aren’t the second and third-class disciples looking at Baek Cheon in anticipation of what he would say?\nHow could he sit back down when so many were watching him? It would turn him into a joke.\nHe let out a low, empty cough a couple times and looked at Chung Myung.\n‘Oh no.’\n‘I need to stop him.’\nJo Gul and Yoon Jong looked at each other.\nIt was hard to beat Chung Myung with a sword, but it was even harder to beat him with words. This guy had no sense of logic. He would resort to all sorts of sophistry and illogical things if it meant winning.\nIf the conversation continued, Baek Cheon might end up humiliated in front of them. So, Chung Myung had to be stopped.\n“*Kuahaha!* Sasuk! Congratulations on your return!”\n“Isn’t today really great? Since the sasuks have returned from training, it looks like we won’t have any problems in the future.”\nJo Gul and Yoon Jong desperately tried to switch the topic.\nThey didn’t mean to defend Chung Myung. It was simply because they didn’t want to see Mount Hua turn into a mess, with Baek Cheon and Chung Myung fighting on their first meeting.\nBaek Cheon glanced at the two of them.\nReflected in their desperate gaze, Baek Cheon opened his mouth with narrowed eyes.\n“Chung Myung, was it?”\n‘Ah what a persistent guy!’\n‘I don’t know. I don’t know anything.’\nUnderstanding that it was impossible to stop him, the two reached out and grabbed the hem of Chung Myung’s robe.\nPlease, just this once. Just this once in your life, behave politely…\nDid he understand their earnestness? Chung Myung opened his mouth.\n“You must have had a tough time traveling all the way here.”\n*Oh?*\nYoon Jong and Jo Gul were shocked. How long had it been since such pleasantries came out of Chung Myung’s mouth…?\n“You keep asking the same thing over and over. Do you need to go rest?”\n… *ehh*, as if his character could change overnight.\nDamn that consistent bastard!\nBaek Cheon’s eyebrows twitched.\n“I will ask once again. Are you certain that we haven’t met before?”\n“Nope, I’ve never seen you before.”\n“It will be better if you answer honestly.”\n“Hey, I already told you that we’ve never met. Do you think I would deceive you?”\nLooking at his glistening eyes, no one would think he was lying.\nExcept for the third-class disciples.\nAnd Un Geom.\n*Uh*… the second-class disciples might also doubt him. *Uh… Hm*.\n“Really? I’ll know if I ask.”\nBaek Cheon turned towards Un Geom. He didn’t want to do this, but this was the only way he could regain the upper hand.\n“Sasuk. I saw that child in the morning, drinking alcohol at a restaurant in Hua-Um.”\nUn Geom’s eyes twitched.\n“Really?”\n“Yes. As far as I know, it is forbidden for the third-class disciples to leave Mount Hua. However, it is definitely unforgivable for them to leave behind their uniform and travel in plain apparel down to—”\n“It is fine.”\n“Yes, I saw… *huh?*”\nBaek Cheon, who bowed his head with clasped hands, lifted his head.\nDid he hear it wrong again?\nNo, what was up with his ears? He keeps hearing the strangest answers…\n“I said, it is fine.”\nBaek Cheon looked at Un Geom with a shocked face.\nUn Geom was a man who valued manners and discipline above all else. He wasn’t strict simply because he was head of the White Plum Blossom boarding house; his strict personality, which was sensitive to the rules, made him the head of the dorms.\nAnd such a person said that it was fine for a third-class disciple to go down Mount Hua even if it was against the rules?\n“S-sasuk?”\n“That child is allowed to go down Mount Hua without asking for permission.”\n“*Uh*, permission? Who would give a child such permission?”\n“The sect leader granted it to him. Have you got an issue with that?”\n“S-sect… leader? Really?”\nHe was so shocked that he had covered his mouth with both his hands. Hearing the tone of Sasuk’s voice, he knew that asking again would be crossing the line. Somehow, Baek Cheon seemed to be getting off on the wrong foot with everyone today.\n“It doesn’t matter.”\nBaek Cheon turned to Chung Myung with a shocked gaze.\n*Nom-nom-nom-nom.*\n“…”\nChung Myung was quickly moving his chopsticks and inhaling food as if he had no interest in what was happening. Baek Cheon would have been less angry if the child at least looked at him with a sneer, but this indifference was infuriating.\nBaek Cheon bit his lip.\n‘I’ll see this through to the end!’\nHe had no intention of backing down.\nBaek Cheon opened his mouth again, but then a man came in through the entrance.\nIt was the finance head, Hyun Young, wearing his usual cold expression. He strode in and looked around.\n“We greet the elder.”\n“We greet the elder.”\nWhen everyone stood and greeted him, he just waved his hand as if it was annoying.\n“Right. Continue having a good time, I shouldn’t have come. I am only here to check it out. Everyone, eat as much as you want and rest in peace. I am leaving. Un Geom, be sure to keep an eye on them so they don’t play too much.”\n“Yes. Sasuk.”\nBaek Cheon, who was still standing, spoke.\n“I have something to say to the elder.”\nHyun Young, who was about to walk out, turned and looked at him.\n“What is it?”\n“I apologize, but I saw a third-class disciple drinking alcohol down the Hua-Um today.”\n“Alcohol?”\n“Yes!”\nHyun Young’s face contorted.\n“A third-class disciple drinking!? Are you sure you saw it with your own eyes?”\n“Definitely. He denies that it was him, but I surely saw correctly.”\nBaek Cheon slightly covered his face to hide the smile under his hands.\nIt might seem impolite, but that child was the one who provoked him first. So, he had to resort to something like this.\n“Who is the disciple you saw?”\nBaek Cheon turned to Chung Myung. He was pretending to be calm, but Baek Cheon was sure that Chung Myung was dying inside.\n‘Don’t blame me for this.’\nThis is to set the rules straight in Mount Hua.\n“The third-class disciple, Chung Myung.”\n“… Who?”\n“Chung Myung. That one over there.”\nWhen it comes to the elders, they couldn’t know each of the third-class disciples by name, so Baek Cheon pointed to the child.\nHyun Young, who looked at Chung Myung, frowned. Then, opened his mouth in irritation with anger rising up.\n“What about it?”\n“Yes. That Chung Myung drank… *huh?*”\n“What about it?”\n“…”\nWhat?
