Wasn’t this a strange thing? He hated that Baek Cheon, who was next to him, was talking more than Chung Myung, who openly harassed people.

No, that chair was fine.
But all the bottles lying on the floor was shocking.
“Mount Hua’s Divine Dragon!”

Tang Gunak was quite surprised by this.
‘He is actually good at listening.’
He thought this guy was stubborn but he…

“YESSSS! Sword Saint Elder!”

Tang Gunak’s eyes twitched.

The old man immediately looked at Tang Gunak.
Flinch.

“G-Great Uncle. It isn’t…”

“Do you know who this person is?! How dare you!”
“…”
No, you don’t even recognize the Lord of the Tang Family, Uncle! Why did you have to believe that guy’s words and let this happen?
Why are you conscious only when this guy speaks?!
“This! This Man! The Plum Blossom! Plum Blossom Sword Saint! He is the best swordsman of Mount Hua, who has made the Demonic Sect shiver in pain! And he is like a brother to my grandfather Tang Bo! What? Cannot drink? You said No?”

Tang Gunak’s eyes were covered in tears.
This was sad.
It was extremely upsetting.
“How dare you make a foolish remark about drinking alcohol with this person?! This person deserves to drink even within the main halls of Shaolin!”

‘No, Elder. Isn’t that a permission that only Shaolin should give?’

Tang Jo Pyung’s praise of the Plum Blossom Sword Saint would never end,
“The hero of the heavens who defeated countless of those demons!”
“Hehehehe.”
“The greatest sword in the world! Uh? The best in the world! No! In the heavens!”
“Hehehe!”
“And he is the greatest guest of the Sichuan Tang Family! Even our own grandfather or great great great grandfather would never dare say such things to me! That goes against the laws of our family! Should I use my cane to put some sense into you?”
“Hehehe!”
Chung Myung laughed, and seeing this, anger rose in the heart of Tang Gunak.
‘N-No! Why are you like this to me?! Why?’

“Get Shaoxing wine.”
“Yes! Get that for him!”
“Get some bamboo leaf too.”
“Right, that too! No! Bring all the bottles we have! All different kinds!”
When Tang Gunak hesitated, Tang Jo Pyung was wide-eyed,
“Why? Should I do it myself?”
“N-No! Great Uncle! I will bring them all right away!”
“Run!”

“Stay here!”
As the two rushed away, Tang Gunak turned to the sky above the furnace,
‘Where did I go wrong…’
I should have stopped him from impersonating the Plum Blossom Sword Saint. Why did I allow all these things to happen?

“So, still taking some more time?”

“If it were melted, we would have already been done, but it takes more time to melt cold steel compared to iron cubes. Now that we are at the final stages, Elder should help us out.”

“Yes. We need a large amount of power at the end.”

“Yes. It is better to end sooner rather than later. Let’s go.”

He could see why Soso wanted to move to Mount Hua.
“Then…”
That moment…
Rumble!
A loud noise came from within. Tang Gunak looked up in shock and saw white flames rising over the chimney.

“Hold it tighter, you brats!”
“Yes!”
Tang Jo Pyung’s eyes were close to madness.
“Get out!”

“Take the hot steel out!”
Thud! Thud! Thud!

“Kuak!”

The Tang Family members, who were far away, each burst out an exclamation.
Sparks erupted from the molten metal. It was as if a river of flame and metal was flowing down. The heat was so great that even though he moved back, his skin was still red.

“Isn’t it all about letting it cool down now?”

“No. It is said that a sword made with cold steel doesn’t always come out properly. We will have to use some hard, refined steel-making skills, and then we will use some Yunnan steel. Besides, aren’t Mount Hua’s swords thinner than normal swords? There will be enough.”
“Oh? Is that so?”
Chung Myung smiled at the happy news.
‘I was worried, but he is a better craftsman than I thought.’
‘Oh! It is getting cold already!’
Tang Gunak, who was watching this from the side, admired the way the tip was cooling. It was his first time watching a sword be made from cold steel.
“Oh god, such a clear silver shine.”
When molten iron that was poured from a furnace began to cool down, it would normally turn black and rough. It was common for one to pound these blemishes out, cleaning them, and then the sword would shine. But for these swords, the moment they began to cool down, it was already as clear as snow.
