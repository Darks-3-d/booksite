Killing that child was the only way to bring you to your senses!”\nIn an instant, there was a murmur.\nHwang Jongi turned his head.\nThe servants who had gathered there after the commotion began were whispering amongst themselves. You—”\n“*Ahhh*.”\nChung Myung waved his hand and cut off the ongoing speech.\n“Enough. Enough.
